# COMPANION ROUTES — Kess, Momo, Thora, Dana

*Built on the Faelen route frame (7 beats: Recruitment → Wall → Proving Ground → First Truth → Fracture → Full Truth → Choice → Endings). Each beat lists its Bond/story gate, its player choice, the flag it sets, and its mechanical hook. See `faelen-route.md` for the full explanation of the frame.*

**The two rules, restated:** every character has one **lie** and one **truth**, and the route is the distance between them. Their **combat kit mirrors the flaw; their ascended upgrade mirrors the cure.**

---

# KESS — Fox Beastkin *(fast DPS / striker)*

- **Arc:** Letting the house be the family she's been searching for.
- **Lie:** *"If I stop hustling, there's nothing holding me here — I have to earn my place or win back what I lost."*
- **Truth:** *"Home isn't the family you lost or the place you earn. It's the people already beside you."*
- **Combat mirror → cure:** A solo striker who over-extends and never lets anyone cover her → her ascended kit **links off allies**, dealing more damage the more the party acts together. She stops fighting alone.

**Gifts —** Loves: street food, flashy cheap trinkets, stream gear, anything from *her* world (makes her cry), festival tickets. Dislikes: money lectures, pity, being told to slow down, heavy sentimental gifts.

- **Beat 0 — Recruitment** *(story)*: A locally-famous beastkin streamer who does gate-clip content. She talks her way into a room "for the content" and frames it as a business deal. All charm, all hustle.
- **Beat 1 — The Wall** *(Bond 1)*: You find her at 3am not editing — scrolling refugee-registry databases, searching names. She slams the laptop shut and jokes it off. **Choice:** press / patience → `KESS_APPROACH`.
- **Beat 2 — Proving Ground** *(Bond 2 + combat)*: She over-extends chasing a kill, gets swarmed, nearly dies because she won't fall back. Her solo-or-nothing fighting *is* her solo-or-nothing life. Unlocks her **burst-DPS ultimate** (glass cannon). **Choice:** bench her / let her run it → `KESS_LEASH`.
- **Beat 3 — First Truth** *(Bond 3 + date)*: She takes you to a hole-in-the-wall serving (bad) food from her world. Admits the hustle isn't for fame — it's for money, and the money funds a search. Half-reveals lost family → `KESS_SEARCH_KNOWN`.
- **Beat 4 — The Fracture** *(Bond 4 + Act 2)*: A lead surfaces — a name match, far off, maybe dangerous, maybe a scam preying on refugees. She'll spend everything and chase it alone tonight. **Choice:** stop her / go with her / let her go → `KESS_FRACTURE`.
- **Beat 5 — Full Truth** *(Bond 5, gated by stop/join)*: The lead ends in heartbreak or a dead end. She breaks — if she stops searching, she's admitting they're gone, and then she'd be *no one*. Realizes the house became her family while she was looking the other way → `KESS_CONFESSED`.
- **Beat 6 — The Choice** *(Bond 6 + Act 3)*: Keep running, or plant roots. **Payoff:** ascended **link/combo kit** — her damage now scales off ally actions; strongest when she's not alone.
- **Endings:** *True* — roots, union, chosen family, strongest linked. *Bittersweet* — stays but restless, one foot out the door. *Lost* — chases the search forever; her stream quietly goes dark.

# MOMO — Gloamkin *(ranged mage / support)*

- **Arc:** Being needed and being a burden are not the same thing.
- **Lie:** *"My existence is a danger to everyone I love. I should disappear."*
- **Truth:** *"The very thing that made me a target is what lets me protect the people I love."*
- **Combat mirror → cure:** Hides in the backline, and her gate-sensitivity passively **draws stronger enemies** (a liability) → her ascended kit turns that sense into **party-wide foreknowledge** — she sees ambushes coming and pre-empts them. The curse becomes the shield.

**Gifts —** Loves: rare books, a good reading lamp, soft blankets, pressed flowers, the gift of quiet company. Dislikes: crowds, being put on the spot, attention-drawing gifts, pressure to "get out more."

- **Beat 0 — Recruitment** *(story)*: Found living rough and alone — she moves constantly because gates keep opening wherever she stays too long. She begs you *not* to take her in, for your own safety. You do anyway.
- **Beat 1 — The Wall** *(Bond 1)*: She senses a gate before it opens and warns the house, saving everyone — then apologizes for "causing" it. **Choice:** press ("that wasn't your fault") / patience → `MOMO_APPROACH`.
- **Beat 2 — Proving Ground** *(Bond 2 + combat)*: A gate opens *because* she's near; mid-fight she tries to flee to "take the danger away," nearly getting someone hurt. She equates her power with harm. Unlocks her **gate-sense mage ultimate**. **Choice:** hold her in formation / tell her to trust her instincts → `MOMO_TRUST`.
- **Beat 3 — First Truth** *(Bond 3 + date)*: A quiet library night. She explains what she is, and why she's spent her life alone. Half-reveal: she may be tied to *why the gates open* — a mystery thread → `MOMO_SENSE_KNOWN`.
- **Beat 4 — The Fracture** *(Bond 4 + Act 2)*: Something begins hunting her specifically. She resolves to leave and lead it away, sacrificing herself for the house. **Choice:** stop her / go with her / let her go → `MOMO_FRACTURE`.
- **Beat 5 — Full Truth** *(Bond 5, gated by stop/join)*: She confesses she never believed she deserved people — that being *needed* felt impossible when she only ever felt like a hazard. She stayed because, for once, she was wanted for herself, not despite her power → `MOMO_CONFESSED`.
- **Beat 6 — The Choice** *(Bond 6 + Act 3)*: Keep hiding her power, or wield it for the house. **Payoff:** ascended **oracle kit** — predicts enemy moves party-wide; "she draws danger" becomes "she sees danger first."
- **Endings:** *True* — owns her power, union, becomes the party's oracle, cracks the gate mystery. *Bittersweet* — uses it but still flinches, half-healed. *Lost* — leaves to protect everyone; the house is safe, and quieter, and emptier.

# THORA — Orc-kin *(tank / healer)*

- **Arc:** Letting herself be cared for instead of only caring.
- **Lie:** *"I exist to give. I don't get to receive, and I don't deserve a second home."*
- **Truth:** *"Being cared for isn't a debt. It's what home is."*
- **Combat mirror → cure:** Pours protection and healing into everyone but herself, runs dry, and falls → her ascended kit is **reciprocal**: the party can now shield and heal *her*, and when they do she unleashes far greater power. Strongest when she lets herself be held up.

**Gifts —** Loves: spices and ingredients from home, a good knife or pot, seeds to plant, a family recipe, anything clearly *for her*. Dislikes: being waited on (she resists, then needs it), extravagance, being made to feel like a guest.

- **Beat 0 — Recruitment** *(story)*: She adopts the house before anyone agrees to it — cooking, mending, mothering. Impossible to refuse.
- **Beat 1 — The Wall** *(Bond 1)*: She never eats until everyone's fed, takes the smallest room, refuses upgrades. You catch her grieving over a keepsake; she instantly busies herself. **Choice:** press / patience → `THORA_APPROACH`.
- **Beat 2 — Proving Ground** *(Bond 2 + combat)*: She keeps the whole party standing, ignores her own wounds, and collapses — because she never healed herself once. Unlocks her **tank/heal ultimate**. **Choice:** force her to self-heal / trust her triage → `THORA_TRIAGE`.
- **Beat 3 — First Truth** *(Bond 3 + date)*: Cooking together. She speaks of the clan she led and lost. Half-reveal: she treats surviving them as a failure she has to keep paying for → `THORA_GRIEF_KNOWN`.
- **Beat 4 — The Fracture** *(Bond 4 + Act 2)*: An echo of her past — a chance to save others at great risk, and she means to spend her own life as the acceptable cost. **Choice:** stop her / go with her / let her go → `THORA_FRACTURE`.
- **Beat 5 — Full Truth** *(Bond 5, gated by stop/join)*: She admits she caretakes *so she never has to be cared for* — because being held would mean admitting she's still here and her people aren't. She lets herself be held for the first time → `THORA_CONFESSED`.
- **Beat 6 — The Choice** *(Bond 6 + Act 3)*: Let the house be *her* home too, not just one she keeps for others. **Payoff:** ascended **reciprocal kit** — protected and healed by the party, she becomes a powerhouse.
- **Endings:** *True* — accepts the second home, union, strongest when protected. *Bittersweet* — softer, but still gives more than she takes. *Lost* — spends herself for others and moves on; the house stays fed and warm and grieving her.

# DANA — Human *(late wildcard / non-combat → game-changer)*

*The foil route. It bends the frame on purpose — her "battles" are bureaucratic until the very end, and she awakens late, like the player.*

- **Arc:** From institutional distance to genuine belonging (the grounded human romance).
- **Lie:** *"The rules keep everyone safe. Attachment is a liability, and the two worlds can't really mix."*
- **Truth:** *"The rules were a wall to hide behind. Connection across the divide is the only thing that actually works."*
- **Combat mirror → cure:** Starts entirely *outside* the fight — an obstacle, an observer → **awakens mana late** and becomes a **wildcard** whose kit doesn't fit the existing roles and rewrites party synergy. The two worlds mixing produces something new.

**Gifts —** Loves: good coffee, a decent pen, mystery novels, a home-cooked meal (cracks her armor), anything that respects her competence. Dislikes: bribes (insulting), over-familiarity too early, sob stories she reads as manipulation, chaos.

- **Beat 0 — Recruitment (as foil)** *(story)*: Assigned to audit your unregistered house full of Gatekin. Adversarial, by-the-book, suspicious of you.
- **Beat 1 — The Wall** *(Bond 1)*: She shows up for "inspections" far more than the job requires — she has nowhere warmer to be. She quietly bends a rule to stop a tenant's relocation, then denies it. **Choice:** press / patience → `DANA_APPROACH`.
- **Beat 2 — Proving Ground (inverted)** *(Bond 2 + story, no combat)*: Her battle is bureaucratic. When superiors move against the house, she chooses the rulebook or the people — and covers for you at professional risk. She hides behind rules to avoid the terror of choosing. **Choice:** thank her openly / give her deniability → `DANA_COVER`.
- **Beat 3 — First Truth** *(Bond 3 + after-hours)*: Off the record. The by-the-book act is armor — she's watched the system fail both worlds and decided caring was a luxury she couldn't afford. Half-reveal: a case that broke her → `DANA_ARMOR_KNOWN`.
- **Beat 4 — The Fracture** *(Bond 4 + Act 2)*: Ordered to shut the house down. Complying keeps her safe and employed; instead she plans to resign and vanish rather than be the one to do it — running from the choice again. **Choice:** stop her / stand with her against it / let her go → `DANA_FRACTURE`.
- **Beat 5 — Full Truth** *(Bond 5, gated by stop/stand)*: She confesses she stopped believing the two worlds could mix because believing it once cost her everything — and the house made her hope again, and hope terrifies her → `DANA_CONFESSED`.
- **Beat 6 — The Choice** *(Bond 6 + Act 3)*: She picks the house over the system — and in the climax, under mortal threat, she **awakens**. **Payoff:** the wildcard kit — late mana, off-role abilities, a game-changer who rewrites how the party fights. Mechanically *is* "the two worlds mixing."
- **Endings:** *True* — throws in fully, awakens, union, the human bridge between worlds. *Bittersweet* — stays and helps from inside the system, never fully crosses over. *Lost* — follows orders or resigns and vanishes; the house survives, but the door to the human world quietly closes.

## Notes for reuse

- **Beat 4 is always the ending engine.** Every route's `_FRACTURE` flag (stop / join / release) is the single choice that weighs most on the outcome. Keep that consistent — players learn the grammar and feel the weight.
- **Dana proves the frame flexes.** If a character isn't a fighter, their Proving Ground and Choice can be social/narrative instead of combat, and the "ascended kit" can be a *new* role entering the game. Use that for any non-combat or late-joining character.
- **Cross-links matter.** Momo's gate-sense and Faelen's Warden knowledge both feed the main mystery — their high-Bond routes should unlock pieces of it, rewarding players who invest deeply with the story's biggest answers.
